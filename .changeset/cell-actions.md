Add cell actions to React and Vue columns. `cellActions` draws small controls after a cell's value (an icon or a label, an optional count, a link or a click handler, revealed always or on hover) and `copyable` adds a copy action. They take the density's control size and text in the grid, List, Kanban and Gallery views, and keep their clicks away from the row, inline editing and drag. Hosts no longer need buttons in a `cellRenderer`, which ignored the density.
