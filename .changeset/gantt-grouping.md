Group the Gantt timeline by one column in React and Vue. The view's first grouping level (from the view menu, saved views, URLs or the host's default) heads each group with the column's value as the table shows it, a task count and a read-only bar spanning its scheduled tasks; groups collapse independently of parent tasks. With `planning.hierarchy`, root tasks are grouped and their descendants stay under them; dependencies to tasks in a collapsed group are hidden like those of collapsed children.
