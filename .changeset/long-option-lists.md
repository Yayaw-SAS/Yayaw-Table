Long option lists stay instant in both editions. A `select` or `multiSelect` with more than 200 options opens the searchable list without `searchable: true` (catalogue forms and the inline single-select editor), and every searchable list and quick-filter picker draws its first 100 matches with a line saying how many more typing can reach. New shared exports: `LONG_OPTION_LIST`, `OPTION_RENDER_LIMIT`, `limitOptions()`.
