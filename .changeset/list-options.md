Add List view options in React and Vue, in `table.list` and saved with views: `wrap` (title on several lines), `showActions` (row actions menu), `propertyAlign` (`"end"` or `"start"`, after the title), `maxProperties`, and `mobileMaxProperties` for screens narrower than 768px. The List settings panel offers each of them.
